## Intro

This document covers experiments replacing the ResNet-50 backbone with ResNet-101 (input resolution 1408×512, pretrained on nuImages with cascade-RCNN). The R101 backbone represents the most straightforward architectural upgrade available — larger backbone, higher input resolution, stronger feature extraction — without requiring any changes to the SparseDrive detection/planning head architecture. Two R101 stage2 variants were evaluated: with and without map head in stage2. Stage1 and stage2 R101 checkpoints were both run. Experiments completed 2026-03-12.

## Method

**Stage1 ablations** (100 epochs, 4GPU or 8GPU): R101 base and R101+DN configurations, identical to the R50 ablation approach but with ResNet-101 backbone at 1408×512 input.

**Stage2 evaluations**:
- `sparsedrive_r101_stage2_4gpu`: R101 backbone, standard stage2 with map head active, 4GPU DGX
- `sparsedrive_r101_stage2_4gpu_nomap`: same as above with map head removed from stage2

Both compared against the DGX R50 bs24 baseline (NDS=0.5232, mAP=0.4132, AMOTA=0.3776).

## Discussion

- **R101 is the single largest lever available in this codebase**: the backbone upgrade from R50→R101 delivers +0.063 NDS, +0.082 mAP, +0.125 AMOTA, and −40% collision rate. No other single change comes close to this magnitude of improvement.
- **R101+nomap is the best R101 configuration**: removing the map head from stage2 further improves NDS (+0.008), mAP (+0.004), AMOTA (+0.001), car ADE (−0.008), and planning L2 (−0.006) vs. R101 with map. The same nomap benefit seen with R50 generalizes to R101.
- **Map quality is preserved with R101**: map mAP holds at 0.5506 vs. 0.5528 for R50 — the map head benefits from better backbone features but the net improvement is small, consistent with map quality being more sensitive to architectural issues (decouple_attn, per-GPU BS) than backbone capacity.
- **AMOTA gain is extraordinary**: +0.125 AMOTA (0.3776 → 0.5020). Stronger backbone features dramatically improve temporal instance stability and reduce ID switches by 44%.
- **DN further improves R101 stage1**: +0.014 NDS, +0.014 mAP, +0.042 AMOTA over R101 base stage1. The DN pattern holds across backbone sizes.
- **Priority**: R101 should be the backbone for all serious performance-optimized experiments. The cost is approximately 2× compute vs. R50.
  - **Retracted for planning (2026-07-25).** This does not hold under the current
    stage-2 architectures: at matched hyperparameters R101 is at parity or
    marginally worse on planning L2 while still costing ~2× compute. The
    recommendation stands only where detection / tracking / map quality is the
    objective. See `reports/2026_07_25_r101_backbone_revisit.md`.

## Future Work

- Run the full R101 stack: `R101 + DN + nomap + rotaug` stage1, followed by `nomap + queue=6 + num_det=100 + 15 epochs` stage2. Expected: NDS≈0.62+, AMOTA≈0.55+, L2≈0.52-0.55, obj_box_col≈0.060-0.075%.
- Benchmark R101 runtime (FPS) vs. R50 to verify latency remains within real-time constraints.
- Test R101 with the full Stage1 ablation stack (DN + nomap + rotaug) to determine if the `+0.031 NDS` cumulative stage1 gain seen with R50 also transfers to R101.
- Consider ViT-based backbone or InternImage as a future architectural step beyond R101.
