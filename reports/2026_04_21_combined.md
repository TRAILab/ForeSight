## Introduction

Three independent lines of improvement have each been validated individually against the SparseDrive stage-2 baseline (`sparsedrive_r50_stage2_4gpu_bs24`, L2=0.636, CR=0.133%):

**Planning head refinement** (`plan_refine` batch, 2026-04-02). Multi-stage cumulative planning refinement combined with endpoint deformable attention and ego instance feature injection (`planpredtrajdeformmm_planinstfeat_laststage`) achieves L2=0.522, CR=0.047% — an 18% reduction in L2 and 65% reduction in collision rate. The mechanism is straightforward: attending at predicted far-horizon trajectory endpoints gives the model direct image evidence of what lies along the planned path, rather than at the current ego position. Restricting the ego instance feature injection to the final decoder stage captures temporal tracking context without suppressing the obstacle-aware attention established in earlier stages.

**3D rotation augmentation** (`rot3d` batch, 2026-04-15). Fixing label-frame consistency in `BBoxRotation` (rot3dv2) — rotating map geometry, agent/ego future trajectories, and ego velocity alongside the 3D boxes — makes rotation augmentation effective end-to-end for the first time. Applied at stage-2 with the public pretrain, rot3dv2 achieves L2=0.593 (−6.8% vs. baseline). The augmentation is orthogonal to the planning head architecture: it improves robustness to scene orientation, which should complement the endpoint attention mechanism.

**Auxiliary 2D supervision** (`aux2d` batch, 2026-04-15). Training with an auxiliary image-space detection head at FPN level 0 substantially improves stage-1 feature quality. The resulting pretrain (`ptaux2d`) combined with `planpredtrajdeformmm` achieves L2=0.560–0.562, CR=0.037–0.062% — a 12% L2 reduction and 53–72% collision reduction over baseline. The aux-2D benefit flows entirely through improved camera feature quality: the stage-2 architecture is unchanged.

Because the three improvements operate at distinct levels — the stage-2 augmentation pipeline, the stage-2 planning decoder architecture, and the stage-1 backbone training objective — their effects are expected to be largely additive. No component modifies the same pathway as another: rot3dv2 operates on the data augmentation before any model forward pass; the planning head changes operate on the decoder query and attention mechanism; aux2d operates on the stage-1 FPN feature representation.

This report documents a systematic combination of all three, starting with the two most mature components (planning refinement and rotation augmentation) and progressively adding aux2d pretrain and, if validated, map DN.

## Method

All three configs are derived from `planpredtrajdeformmm_planinstfeat_laststage` (the best model from the `plan_refine` batch) with two orthogonal modifications applied independently and jointly.

**`ppdeformmm_planifls_rot3dv2`** adds rot3dv2 stage-2 augmentation (`rot3d_range=[-0.3925, 0.3925]`, ±22.5°) to the laststage planning head. Pretrain: public `sparsedrive_stage1.pth`.

**`ptaux2d_ppdeformmm_planifls`** upgrades the pretrain to `sparsedrive_stage1_aux2d.pth` (from the aux2d stage-1 run), replacing the public checkpoint. No augmentation change.

**`ptaux2d_ppdeformmm_planifls_rot3dv2`** applies both changes simultaneously: aux2d pretrain + rot3dv2 augmentation.
