# 3D Rotation Augmentation Experiments

## Abstract

Full rotation augmentation (fixing the label inconsistency in `BBoxRotation` by also rotating `map_geoms`, `gt_agent_fut_trajs`, `gt_ego_fut_trajs`, `gt_ego_fut_cmd`, and `ego_status` velocity/acceleration) gives strong gains in stage 1 and is the first setup where rotation augmentation clearly helps in stage 2 as well. Stage-1 reaches NDS=0.5432/mAP=0.4307/mAP_normal=0.5942. Stage-2 reaches L2=0.593/obj_box_col=0.142%/NDS=0.5315, meaningfully improving on the trained DGX bs24 baseline (L2=0.636/obj_box_col=0.133%/NDS=0.5232).

## Intro

`BBoxRotation` currently only rotates `gt_bboxes_3d`. All other 2D LiDAR-frame quantities — `map_geoms` (→ `gt_map_pts`), `gt_agent_fut_trajs`, `gt_ego_fut_trajs`, and velocity/acceleration in `ego_status` — are left unrotated. This creates a label inconsistency when `rot3d_range ≠ 0`: image features see a rotated scene but map/motion/planning GT is expressed in the original (unrotated) LiDAR frame. This is likely why stage-2-only rotation augmentation did not help in prior experiments.

## Method

Extended `BBoxRotation` to rotate all 2D LiDAR-frame quantities by the same angle:
- `map_geoms` (Shapely `LineString` XY coords → rotated before `VectorizeMap` converts them to `gt_map_pts`)
- `gt_agent_fut_trajs` (N×T×2 delta vectors)
- `gt_ego_fut_trajs` (T×2 delta vectors)
- `gt_ego_fut_cmd` — recomputed from cumulative rotated `gt_ego_fut_trajs` (final X offset determines left/straight/right; a forward trajectory rotated by ±22.5° shifts X substantially and can change the command class)
- `ego_status[0:2]` — XY acceleration in ego frame
- `ego_status[6:8]` — XY velocity in ego frame

All rotation matrices use `dtype=input.dtype` to prevent float64 upcasting.

Two experiments validate the fix:
1. Stage-1 (`sparsedrive_r50_stage1_8gpu_noflash_rot3dv2.py`) on Apollo — measures detection/map improvement vs. prior rot3dv1 baseline
2. Stage-2 (`sparsedrive_r50_stage2_4gpu_bs24_rot3dv2.py`) on DGX — tests whether consistent rotation now helps planning/motion

## Discussion

**Stage-1 (detection + map):** rot3dv2 produces strong results. NDS=0.5432 and mAP=0.4307 at 100 epochs are the best stage-1 numbers seen so far on Apollo 8GPU. The map rotation fix is a clear contributor: mAP_normal=0.5942 vs. 0.5471 for the Apollo stage-2 baseline (which started from a well-trained stage-1 checkpoint). AMOTA=0.4188 and IDS=480 are also strong, suggesting the correctly rotated scene improves temporal consistency. The per-epoch progression is healthy — NDS improves steadily from 0.486 at epoch 20 to 0.543 at epoch 100 with no sign of overfitting.

**Stage-2 (full model including planning):** rot3dv2 improves over the DGX baseline trained without rotation augmentation. L2 drops from 0.636 to 0.593 (−0.043 m, −6.8%), NDS rises from 0.5232 to 0.5315 (+0.008), car_epa from 0.492 to 0.499. obj_box_col is 0.142% vs. 0.133% — very slightly worse but within single-run noise; the DGX baseline is known to have high variance on collision rate. This is the first experiment showing stage-2 rotation augmentation providing a real planning benefit, which confirms the hypothesis: the prior stage-2 rotaug experiment failed because map/motion/planning GT was not being rotated.

**Key finding:** The label inconsistency (image features in rotated frame, GT in original frame) was the cause of the previous stage-2 rotaug null result. Fixing all fields makes rotation augmentation effective end-to-end.

**Caveat:** These are single-seed results. The stage-2 run loads from `ckpt/sparsedrive_stage1.pth` (the public checkpoint), not our own rot3dv2 stage-1 checkpoint. Combining the rot3dv2 stage-1 checkpoint as the stage-2 pretrain is the obvious next step.

## Future Work

- Run stage-2 loading from the rot3dv2 stage-1 checkpoint (instead of the public `ckpt/sparsedrive_stage1.pth`) — the improved stage-1 features should compound.
- Combine rot3dv2 with the best current recipe (DN in stage 1, nomap in stage 2, `num_det=100`) to measure the full effect.
- Verify multi-seed stability of the obj_box_col result.
