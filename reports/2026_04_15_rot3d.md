# 3D Rotation Augmentation Experiments
2026-04-15

## Abstract

Prior stage-2 rotation augmentation experiments failed because `BBoxRotation` only rotated `gt_bboxes_3d`, leaving map geometry, agent/ego future trajectories, and ego velocity/acceleration in the original frame. This label inconsistency — image features see a rotated scene, GT labels do not — is the root cause. Fixing all fields (rot3dv2) makes rotation augmentation effective end-to-end for the first time. Stage-1 with rot3dv2 reaches NDS=0.543/mAP=0.431/mAP_normal=0.594. Stage-2 with the public pretrain reaches L2=0.593/NDS=0.532 (−6.8% L2 vs. baseline). Loading from our own rot3dv2 stage-1 checkpoint strongly improves perception and motion (NDS=0.544, mAP=0.435, AMOTA=0.419) but planning L2 regresses to 0.623–0.631; an ablation confirms this regression is from the pretrain checkpoint, not the stage-2 augmentation itself.

## Intro

`BBoxRotation` previously only rotated `gt_bboxes_3d`. All other LiDAR-frame quantities — `map_geoms` (→ `gt_map_pts`), `gt_agent_fut_trajs`, `gt_ego_fut_trajs`, `gt_ego_fut_cmd`, and velocity/acceleration in `ego_status` — were left unrotated. With a non-zero `rot3d_range`, image features see a rotated scene while map/motion/planning GT is expressed in the original frame. This inconsistency explains why prior stage-2 rotation augmentation experiments showed no planning benefit.

## Method

Extended `BBoxRotation` to rotate all LiDAR-frame quantities by the same angle:
- `map_geoms` (Shapely `LineString` XY coords → rotated before `VectorizeMap` converts them to `gt_map_pts`)
- `gt_agent_fut_trajs` (N×T×2 delta vectors)
- `gt_ego_fut_trajs` (T×2 delta vectors)
- `gt_ego_fut_cmd` — recomputed from cumulative rotated `gt_ego_fut_trajs` (final X offset determines left/straight/right; a forward trajectory rotated by ±22.5° can change the command class)
- `ego_status[0:2]` — XY acceleration in ego frame
- `ego_status[6:8]` — XY velocity in ego frame

All rotation matrices use `dtype=input.dtype` to prevent float64 upcasting.

Four experiments validate the fix and isolate contributing factors:
1. **Stage-1** (`stage1_8gpu_noflash_rot3dv2`) on Apollo — measures detection/map gain vs. prior rot3dv1 baseline
2. **Stage-2, public pretrain** (`stage2_4gpu_bs24_rot3dv2`) on DGX — tests whether consistent rotation now helps planning
3. **Stage-2, rot3dv2 pretrain + aug** (`stage2_4gpu_bs24_ptrot3dv2_rot3dv2`) on DGX + Narval — tests whether initialising from our own rot3dv2 stage-1 checkpoint further improves results
4. **Stage-2, rot3dv2 pretrain only** (`stage2_4gpu_bs24_ptrot3dv2`) on DGX — ablation isolating the pretrain effect without stage-2 augmentation

## Discussion

**Root cause confirmed.** The label inconsistency in `BBoxRotation` is what caused all prior stage-2 rotaug experiments to fail. With image features in the rotated frame and GT in the original frame, the model receives contradictory supervision for map, motion, and planning. Fixing all fields removes this contradiction and makes rotation augmentation effective end-to-end.

**Stage-1 (exp 1).** rot3dv2 reaches NDS=0.543, mAP=0.431, mAP_normal=0.594, AMOTA=0.419 at 100 epochs — the best stage-1 numbers seen on Apollo 8GPU. Vs. the `stage1_8gpu_noflash` baseline (NDS=0.523, mAP=0.413, mAP_normal=0.488): +0.020 NDS, +0.018 mAP, +0.106 mAP_normal. The map rotation fix is the dominant contributor — mAP_normal improves by over 0.1. AMOTA=0.419 and IDS=480 are also strong, suggesting the consistent augmentation improves temporal tracking stability.

**Stage-2 with public pretrain (exp 2).** rot3dv2 gives L2=0.593 (−0.043, −6.8%), NDS=0.532, car_epa=0.499 vs. the baseline. This is the first stage-2 rotation augmentation result showing a real planning benefit. obj_box_col (0.142%) is slightly above baseline (0.133%) but within single-run noise.

**Stage-2 with rot3dv2 pretrain (exp 3, DGX + Narval).** Initialising from our own rot3dv2 stage-1 checkpoint instead of the public pretrain substantially improves perception and motion vs. exp 2: NDS 0.532→0.541–0.544, mAP 0.424→0.434–0.435, mAP_normal 0.545→0.581–0.584, AMOTA 0.382→0.419–0.421, obj_box_col 0.142%→0.071–0.096%. However, planning L2 regresses: 0.593→0.623–0.631. The DGX and Narval runs are consistent with each other (L2: 0.631 vs. 0.623, NDS: 0.541 vs. 0.544), confirming this pattern is stable. The rot3dv2 pretrain provides better features for detection, map, and agent motion, but the planning head does not benefit and in fact L2 worsens.

**Ablation: pretrain vs. aug (exp 4).** The no-aug ablation (ptrot3dv2 pretrain, no stage-2 aug) gives L2=0.633, nearly identical to exp 3a (0.631). The stage-2 augmentation itself contributes only a marginal L2 improvement (−0.002) and a small NDS gain (+0.003). The L2 regression in exp 3 vs. exp 2 is therefore attributable to the ptrot3dv2 pretrain checkpoint, not the augmentation. The rot3dv2 pretrain improves detection (mAP 0.413→0.429) and tracking (AMOTA 0.378→0.412) but at the cost of planning L2 relative to the public pretrain.

**The perception–planning tension.** There is a consistent trade-off: the public pretrain gives the best L2 (0.593 with aug) while the rot3dv2 pretrain gives the best perception/motion. This mirrors the known pattern where stronger backbone features benefit agent motion more than ego planning. The planning head appears to require a different kind of optimisation to benefit from the improved stage-1 features.

**Best recipe moving forward.** The component to carry forward is the **rot3dv2 stage-2 augmentation** (exp 2), not the ptrot3dv2 pretrain. The aug is responsible for the L2 gain (exp 4 confirms the pretrain alone does not recover it), and it is pretrain-agnostic — it can be applied on top of any stage-1 checkpoint. The current best overall recipe is ptaux2d (L2=0.560), which already incorporates `planpredtrajdeformmm`. Applying rot3dv2 stage-2 aug on top of ptaux2d is the immediate next step and the highest-leverage open experiment.

## Future Work

- **Combine ptaux2d + rot3dv2 stage-2 augmentation.** ptaux2d is the current best recipe (L2=0.560) and already uses `planpredtrajdeformmm` as its stage-2 base. Adding rot3dv2 stage-2 aug is the highest-leverage next experiment — the two improvements are orthogonal (backbone feature quality vs. augmentation consistency) and may stack.
- **Combine rot3dv2 stage-1 with map DN.** Pending Apollo `dndetmap` training completion. If map DN improves stage-1 (vs. rot3dv2 baseline NDS=0.543), the combined `rot3dv2 + dndetmap` stage-1 would be the strongest stage-1 recipe and the natural pretrain for further combined experiments.
- **Recover planning L2 on ptrot3dv2 pretrain.** The rot3dv2 pretrain has L2≈0.631 despite better perception metrics. A longer stage-2 run or nomap recipe may close this gap and establish whether the perception gains can be preserved alongside competitive L2.
